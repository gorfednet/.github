# Ship bot incidents

One dated line per incident whose cause was a pattern: what happened, what changed, a link.

- 13 November 2026: the owner's GitHub REST quota (5,000 an hour) ran out at about 09:03 ET and stalled ship-bot and every session until 09:23 ET (it had also run out on 10, 11 and 12 November). Ship-bot shared that quota through `gh auth token` and polled 21 repositories every 2 minutes with uncached REST, re-running the three gate scripts on every waiting pull request each pass. Changed: conditional requests with an ETag cache, gate answers reused while their inputs are unchanged, a stop under 200 remaining with one ntfy alert, and a GitHub App token with its own quota (plan row 1.3, PR on branch `fix/ship-bot-quota`).
- 13 November 2026, after PR 143 was installed: the quota ran out again at about 10:00 ET. Desk's lead was that sessions' `gh run watch` (3 s default) and `gh pr checks --watch` (10 s) were the main consumers. The numbers support it. The installed bot's warm passes charge 5 calls each (37 of 42 answered 304), about 150 an hour, or 3%. Between 10:23 and 10:39 ET the account spent 1,401, a pace of about 5,250 an hour, so sessions spent about 97%. One `gh run watch` at 3 s makes about 40 calls a minute, about 2,400 an hour. Before PR 143 the bot's share was about 1,800 an hour (36%). Changed: `--interval 60` in `~/.claude/rules/shipping.md` (Desk). The bot moves to its own quota when the GitHub App is installed.
- 13 November 2026: papers.4thcltr.com 35 merged, then its `verify` setup step (`make verify`, the full Playwright suite on this Mac) failed 7 of 144 specs at 16:33 UTC, all frame-timing wordmark specs, with the 1-minute load at about 11 on 10 cores. The same specs pass at normal load. A setup failure blocks the repository. Changed: a deploy row can say `"needsHeadroom": true`, and ship-bot then holds its merges while the load is above the core count (or can't be read), with a log line, no comment and no block. The papers row has it.
